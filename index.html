About Me

I’m Ritul, founder of Xondhan, a digital solutions brand from Assam. I build business websites, management systems, automation and AI-powered tools that turn ideas into practical, working solutions. I focus on understanding what clients truly need, not just what they ask for, but the problems and goals behind the request. That helps me simplify complex requirements, communicate clearly and build solutions that give clients more confidence and control over their work. At Xondhan, a project is more than a finished product. It is about listening, solving the right problem and building trust through reliability, transparency and meaningful results. My long-term vision is to grow Xondhan into a trusted digital solutions brand that helps businesses and organisations move forward with confidence. Understanding people. Solving real problems.
